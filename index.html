Tell us what you're starting

This can be a project, a plan, or an idea you're working on with other people or groups. It doesn't need a name or a budget yet.

There are about a dozen questions in four short steps: what you're trying to do, what resources you have, who is involved and how, and what is stopping the next step. Rough answers are fine, and you can change anything before you submit.

When you submit, you get a page for your project. It shows:

  • who has committed, who you've consulted, and which of them have confirmed;
  • the next step, and whether the exchange can help with it;
  • people and groups on the exchange who could help, and who you already know who can introduce you.

Sometimes the next step isn't a new contact. It may be a decision, an approval, or agreement from neighbors. When that's the case, the page says so, and a facilitator can help you work through it.

Only you and the exchange's facilitators can see your project page. People you name who are on the exchange are asked to confirm, and they decide whether to. Your budget is visible only to facilitators unless you choose otherwise.
